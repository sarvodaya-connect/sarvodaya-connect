# UN SDG icons

The 17 Sustainable Development Goal icons in `apps/dashboard/public/sdg/` and `apps/mobile/assets/sdg/` (`sdg-01.jpg` … `sdg-17.jpg`) are the official United Nations SDG icons, unchanged, as placed in the Sarvodaya Connect V5 prototype (Figma "Sarvodaya Prototype", SP07 and M51). They were exported from the prototype at 220 × 220 pixels.

- Source: United Nations, Sustainable Development Goals communications materials — https://www.un.org/sustainabledevelopment/news/communications-material/
- Use follows the UN SDG icon guidelines: the icons are shown in their official order 1–17, with their number and full title, and are not altered.
